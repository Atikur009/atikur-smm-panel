Mass Order

How it works

  1. Put one order per line in this exact format: service_id | link | quantity
  2. Every line is checked first — if any line is invalid, nothing is ordered.
  3. The total charge is taken from your balance once, and all orders are created as pending for admin confirmation.

Example: 1 | https://facebook.com/example | 1000
