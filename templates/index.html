Frequently Asked Questions

What is this panel?

An online dashboard where you buy social media growth services — followers, likes, views, subscribers and more — and pay in BDT via bKash.

Do you need my social media password?

Never. Every order only needs your public profile, post or video link. Anyone asking for your password is not us.

How do I add funds?

Send money via bKash (Send Money, not Recharge) to the number on the Add Funds page, then submit the TrxID and your bKash number. Your balance is added after admin approval.

How long until my order starts?

Orders wait for admin confirmation first, then are processed. You can follow the live status of every order in your dashboard.

What if something goes wrong with my order?

Open a support ticket from your dashboard, mention the order number if you have one, and our team will reply there.

Is there a minimum order?

Each service has its own minimum and maximum quantity, shown on its card and on the order form.
